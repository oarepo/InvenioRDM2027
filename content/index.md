**By plane:** Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG) – trolleybus\ 59 to Nádraží Veleslavín, then metro\ A towards Depo Hostivař to Dejvická (about 35\ min, €2; see the [Honest Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=57WeRN0Nrb4)). Alternatively, take an Uber (the official airport taxi).

**Local transport:** The venue is a short walk from Dejvická metro station (line\ A) and the trams at Vítězné náměstí. For tickets and tips, see [Getting around](#getting-around){.inline}.

### Getting around

Metro, trams and buses all use the same tickets. Always carry a valid one – don't risk meeting our not-so-friendly ticket inspectors.

::: {.grid .grid-3}
::: {.card .small}
#### Buy a ticket
In the [PID Lítačka app](https://app.pidlitacka.cz/), or from the vending machines in metro stations and on board trams and buses (near the middle door, card only).
:::

::: {.card .small}
#### Validate paper tickets
Tickets from station machines must be stamped before use. In the metro, the validators are at the station entrance (usually at the top of the escalators); on trams and buses, stamp your ticket as soon as you board.
:::

::: {.card .small}
#### Plan your trip
Find connections in the PID Lítačka app or on [pid.cz](https://pid.cz/en/search-connection/?to=Dejvick%C3%A1) – the planner opens with Dejvická, the stop nearest the venue, already filled in.
:::
:::

### Explore Prague

New to Prague? Before you set out, watch the Honest Guide's [10 Tourist Mistakes You Can Outsmart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FI_31n3gRkg) – it will save you money and hassle.

The official city guides list plenty of places to visit – here are a few of our own favourites. Most sights close before our sessions end each day, so why not stay for the weekend before or after the meeting?

::: {.grid .grid-4}
::: {.card .small}
![Photo: [Jorge Royan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Municipal_House_(Obecni_Dum)_ceiling,_Prague_-_8906.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)](assets/explore/municipal-house.jpg){.card-img data-full="assets/explore/full/municipal-house.jpg" alt="Alfons Mucha's painted ceiling and murals in the Mayor's Hall of the Municipal House"}

#### Municipal House
One of Prague's finest Art Nouveau buildings, filled with decorative details, murals and beautiful interiors – and the patriotic motto "By strength to freedom, by love to harmony". Take a guided tour of the ceremonial rooms, or attend a concert in Smetana Hall.
:::

::: {.card .small}
![Photo: [Jan Slavík](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vzducholo%C4%8F_Gulliver,_DOX_Praha,_2016,_foto_Jan_Slav%C3%ADk.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)](assets/explore/dox-gulliver.jpg){.card-img data-full="assets/explore/full/dox-gulliver.jpg" alt="The wooden Gulliver airship on top of DOX, seen from below"}

#### DOX Centre for Contemporary Art
A former industrial complex transformed into one of Prague's most distinctive contemporary art centres. It hosts thought-provoking exhibitions on art, design, literature and architecture. Don't miss the extraordinary wooden Gulliver airship on its roof.
:::

::: {.card .small}
![Photo: [MONUDET](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Praha,_Vy%C5%A1ehrad,_pevnost,_s%C3%A1l_Gorlice.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)](assets/explore/gorlice.jpg){.card-img data-full="assets/explore/full/gorlice.jpg" alt="Original Baroque statues from Charles Bridge in the underground Gorlice hall"}

#### Vyšehrad Casemates and Gorlice Hall
Explore historic military corridors beneath the fortress and discover a remarkable underground hall housing several of the original Baroque statues from Charles Bridge.
:::

::: {.card .small}
![Photo: [Horakvlado](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sklen%C3%ADk_Fata_Morgana,_Pra%C5%BEsk%C3%A1_botanick%C3%A1_zahrada,_Trojsk%C3%A1_194,_Praha_-_Troja_04.JPG), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)](assets/explore/fata-morgana.jpg){.card-img data-full="assets/explore/full/fata-morgana.jpg" alt="The curved glass Fata Morgana greenhouse"}

#### Botanical Garden
A green escape from the city, with outdoor collections, hillside views and the tropical Fata Morgana greenhouse. Stop by St.\ Clare's Vineyard for a glass of local Prague wine.
:::
