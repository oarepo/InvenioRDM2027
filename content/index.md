## Venue

::: {.grid .grid-2}
::: card
### CESNET
The meeting takes place at the CESNET headquarters in Prague 6 – Dejvice. If you arrive on Monday, there will be people there to meet and greet you. If you arrive later, please ask at the reception and they will direct you.

**Address:** CESNET a.l.e., Generála Píky 430/26, 160 00 Praha 6, Czech Republic

[View on Mapy.com](https://mapy.com/en/zakladni?q=CESNET%2C%20Gener%C3%A1la%20P%C3%ADky%20430%2F26%2C%20Praha%206&x=14.3887&y=50.0988&z=17){.inline}
:::

::: card
### Getting to the Workshop
**By plane:** Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG) – trolleybus 59 to Nádraží Veleslavín, then metro A towards Depo Hostivař to Dejvická (about 35 min, €2; see the [Honest Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=57WeRN0Nrb4)). Alternatively, take an Uber (the official airport taxi).

**By train (from Vienna):** Praha hlavní nádraží (main station) – metro C towards Háje, change at Muzeum to metro A towards Nemocnice Motol and get off at Dejvická.

**By train (from Berlin):** Praha-Holešovice – metro C towards Háje, change at Muzeum to metro A towards Nemocnice Motol and get off at Dejvická.

**Local transport:** The venue is a short walk from metro A station Dejvická and the trams at Vítězné náměstí. Always carry a valid ticket – don't risk meeting our not-so-friendly ticket inspectors. Plan your trip and buy tickets in the [PID Lítačka app](https://app.pidlitacka.cz/){.inline}.

### Explore Prague

::: {.grid .grid-4}
::: {.card .small}
#### Prague Castle
One of the largest castle complexes in the world, with St. Vitus Cathedral and Golden Lane. About a 15-minute walk from the venue.
:::

::: {.card .small}
#### Charles Bridge
The 14th-century stone bridge over the Vltava, lined with 30 baroque statues. Come early in the morning to beat the crowds.
:::

::: {.card .small}
#### Old Town Square
Home of the medieval Astronomical Clock and the Týn Church. Three stops from Dejvická on metro A (Staroměstská).
:::

::: {.card .small}
#### Vyšehrad
A hilltop fortress with great views over the river and the cemetery where Dvořák and Smetana rest. Metro C to Vyšehrad.
:::
